Principles of Compounding
  • Start Early: Time in the market is significantly more impactful than timing the market.
  • Consistency Wins: Systematic monthly contributions dollar-cost average market fluctuations.
  • Reinvest Yields: Compounding exponentially accelerates as interest earns interest on itself.
